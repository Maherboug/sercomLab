SERCOM Lab Scientific Day

A Day of Innovation, Collaboration, and Vision

About the Day

This Scientific Day serves as a vibrant forum for the Lab's team to present and share groundbreaking contributions in various IT fields. These include distributed computing, data science, security, communication systems, and IoT. The event is also an opportunity to welcome new members, foster teamwork, and define future strategies.
