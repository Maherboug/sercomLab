🚀 Hack for Safer Roads – Smart Mobility Hackathon 🚀

About the Event

Hack for Road Safety is a 24-hour innovation hackathon open to students, startups, and researchers. Whether you're a tech enthusiast, an IoT innovator, a data analyst, or simply passionate about finding creative solutions to improve road safety, this event is for you. Join us in developing cutting-edge ideas and technologies to save lives on Tunisia's roads!

📍 Location: École Polytechnique de Tunisie
📅 Date: 26–27 April 2025
